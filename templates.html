Before you send

A few habits that keep every email accurate, complete and appropriate for its audience.

👥

Recipients are roles, not addresses

Each template lists who it is for by role — for example “Customer technical contact” or “HOTO Manager”. Add the real people yourself: Open in email starts a new email with only the subject and body filled in.

📎

Complete the brackets, add the attachments

Anything in [square brackets] is a note for you: replace it or delete it. Attach what the email mentions — the UAT document, the device list for the CMDB, or the SharePoint link for the HOTO document.

🔒

Customer-facing or internal

Customer-facing drafts avoid internal tool names such as GOLD, MACHX, FLIP and ServiceNow, and explain terms like UAT, HOTO and CAB. Drafts tagged Internal — for example the CMDB, DNAC and HOTO requests — are for Orange Business teams only.

📅

Meeting invites

Templates tagged Meeting invite are written for a Teams or Outlook meeting. Create the meeting, paste the subject as its title and the body as its description, then add the attendees — on migration invites, the PM and SC go in as optional.

🛠️

Maintaining these templates. All the wording lives in templates-data.js: each template has a title, the process step it serves, its recipients by role, a subject and a body, with {{placeholders}} that match the fields under Your details. When the team agrees an official version, paste it in and set draft: false to remove its Draft tag. Each template quotes its step from the AP, WLC & Switch and WAN process pages; the RACI matrix shows who owns each step.
