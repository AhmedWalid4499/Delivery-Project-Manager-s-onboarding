📦 DPM Tool

HOTO Pack Builder

Check the hand-over pack is complete — runbook, LLD, UAT sign-off, CMDB & DNAC updates, success notification, GOLD/SALTO closed — then build the LAN or WAN HOTO tracker and export the pack to send to the HOTO Manager.

🔒

Private to you. Everything here is saved only in this browser on this device — nothing is sent anywhere. It reads your projects from the Delivery Checklist (also stored only in this browser).

⚠️

Choose a project

Pick one of your delivery-checklist projects. The builder loads its service (LAN or WAN), and auto-ticks the readiness items you have already completed on the checklist.

Is the pack complete?

Each item is Done, Outstanding or N/A. Items marked from your checklist tick automatically from the project's progress — you can still override them here.

Site / device tracker

Fill in one row per device (LAN) or per site / circuit (WAN). Add, duplicate or delete rows. Everything is saved only in this browser.

Build & hand over

Export the HOTO pack as an Excel workbook (summary, tracker and readiness), copy a short hand-over note, and open the email template for the HOTO Manager.

✉️ HOTO email template

The workbook has three sheets — HOTO Summary, HOTO Tracker and Readiness. Send it, with the HOTO document, to the HOTO Manager for sign-off.
