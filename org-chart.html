How the team is organised

The structure in a minute — where Europe DPM sits, how the clusters work, and who the SMEs are.

Europe Delivery Project Management is part of Order to Bill, headed by Khaled Khattab. Mona Tamimy leads the Europe DPMs, and the team beneath her is organised into regional clusters, each one run by its own cluster manager.

🏛️
Part of Order to Bill

The reporting line runs from the Head of Order to Bill, to the Head of Europe DPMs, to the cluster managers.

🗺️
Regional clusters

The team is split by region. Each cluster covers its own territory and has a manager who leads the DPMs in it.

🤝
Peers within a cluster

Inside a cluster, DPMs work side by side as peers and pick up deliveries anywhere across the cluster's territory.

🧠
Some DPMs are also SMEs

A few DPMs double as Subject Matter Experts, the go-to people for deeper technical or process questions. They carry a DPM / SME tag.
