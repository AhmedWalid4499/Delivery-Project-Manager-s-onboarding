The RACI Matrix

Delivery is a team sport, but ownership must be unambiguous. This matrix shows who is Accountable and Responsible for each activity in a typical delivery — and where you consult or simply inform.

How to read it

Each activity has exactly one Accountable owner. Where the same party both owns and does the work, the cell shows A/R.

The DPM golden rule

The DPM is accountable for the delivery end-to-end — the dates, the MACHX raises, the Field Engineer dispatch, and the HOTO package are yours. The PM owns scope, BOM and the commercial relationship; the SC owns the design (runbook & LLD). Keeping these boundaries clear is what keeps a delivery calm.
