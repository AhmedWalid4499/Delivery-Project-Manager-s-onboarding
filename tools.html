🚀 Launchpad

Your tools, one click away

Every system a DPM uses on a delivery — from the GOLD order to the HOTO paperwork — and what each one is for.

The tools you'll use

Grouped the same way as the handbook's Tools & Systems section. Open each from your own bookmarks or the intranet — the internal addresses aren't published here.

Core delivery tools

📦

GOLD

Order management — where the customer order lives. You track it and close the GOLD order at the end of delivery.

🎫

ServiceNow (SNOW)

Change requests and CMDB updates (the record of what's installed) — change control around migration windows.

🛠️

MACHX

Raise and manage TIM / VPO work orders that get engineering & staging done. An AP job needs one raise; a Switch/WLC job needs two.

👷

FLIP

Book Field Engineers to attend site — used on Switch/WLC deliveries where hardware is handled on-site.

📡

DNAC / Catalyst Center

Cisco's network management platform. After migration you send the DNAC update so the new devices are managed and monitored.

🧾

SALTO

The second order system — closed post-migration alongside GOLD.

📁

SharePoint

Where delivery documents live — upload the HOTO document, runbook and LLD (Low-Level Design) for each project.

Reporting, time & automation

⏱️

Oracle PRIME

Time entry — logging your hours against projects.

📊

Power BI

Dashboards and reporting on delivery status and KPIs.

🤖

Power Automate / Office Scripts

Automating repetitive steps (reminders, data prep) — much of it built by the Automation squad.

Quick mental model

Six ideas that tell you which tool to open. From the DPM Onboarding Handbook, Tools & Systems.

  • 📦
    GOLD / SALTO
    = the order
  • 🛠️
    ServiceNow / MACHX
    = getting the work approved & done
  • 👷
    FLIP
    = people on site
  • 📡
    DNAC
    = managing the result
  • 📁
    SharePoint
    = the paperwork
  • 📊
    PRIME / Power BI
    = your time & the reporting
🧭
