Delivery Planner

Enter a site's details and where each activity is up to, and the planner estimates the dates and the go-live, draws a live Gantt chart, and exports a shareable Excel plan for the customer — all in working days, all in your browser.

🔒

Saved only in this browser. This plan is kept on this device only (dpmkb_planner_v1) — nothing is sent anywhere. Export the Excel to share it with the customer.

Plan details

Describe the site and when the plan starts. The Gantt and the dates count forward from the start, in working days (Mon–Fri).

Activities & dates

Seed the activities from a service template, then edit freely. Mark each one Done, In progress or Not started — the dates, the go-live estimate and the Gantt update as you type.

Gantt preview

A day-by-day view of the plan. Bars are coloured by status; weekends are shaded and today is marked. It scrolls sideways — the Excel export looks the same.

Share & export

Export a customer-ready Excel, print or save a clean copy, or copy a short plain-text summary for an email or Teams post.
