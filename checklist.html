Delivery Checklist

Turn the AP, WLC & Switch and WAN processes into a live checklist for each delivery — enter the project details, tick steps as you go, see what's next, and export a formatted Excel plan with a Gantt chart.

🔒

Private to you. Your projects are stored only in this browser on this device — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Back up (JSON) to move them to another browser.

⚠️

Enter the project details first — then work through its checklist below. Only the name is required; you can edit everything later.

Your projects

One checklist per delivery, each with its own ticks, notes and dates. Projects in progress are listed soonest target date first; completed ones move to the end.

Checklist

Work top to bottom — the first open step is highlighted as Next up. Ticks are saved as you go. Open Why this step? under a step for a plain-English explanation.

The three checklists

Each checklist mirrors its process page step for step, with the teams, owners and tools shown on every step.

💡

Good to know. Export to Excel downloads a formatted workbook for the open project — its details, every step with an explanation, and a plan with a Gantt chart that starts on the kick-off date. Copy status update puts a plain-text summary — progress by phase and the next three open steps — on your clipboard, ready for an email or Teams post. Print gives a clean paper or PDF copy of the open checklist. Step wording follows the process pages; if a step is reworded later, your ticks stay put.
