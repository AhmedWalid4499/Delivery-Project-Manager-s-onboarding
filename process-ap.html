📡 LAN Process — Wireless

AP Migration Process

Orange Business — Delivery Project Manager end-to-end process guide. Built from the MLAN (AP) project plan.

📡 What is an AP Migration?

An AP (Access Point) migration is the process of replacing old wireless access points at a customer site with new Cisco Catalyst APs managed by a Wireless LAN Controller (WLC). As the DPM, your job is not to do the technical work yourself — your job is to coordinate all the people and steps that need to happen in the right order so the customer's WiFi stays working and the project closes cleanly.

The AP flavour of the LAN process has one defining trait: the customer mounts the APs themselves and the VPO stages them remotely — so there is no Field Engineer on site and only one MACHX raise. The one thing that must never be skipped is verifying DHCP Option 43.

🤔 Why does it need a process?

Multiple teams are involved — the ODM and Orange supply chain (ordering & logistics), the Solution Consultant (runbook & LLD), the VPO who stages the APs, the customer who mounts them, and the DHCP / change teams. If any step is missed or done out of order (like forgetting Option 43), the APs won't connect to the WLC and the migration fails.

✅ What does success look like?

All new APs are online and broadcasting every customer SSID. The customer has confirmed every service works. CMDB and DNAC are updated, the GOLD and SALTO orders are closed, and the HOTO document (with runbook and LLD) is uploaded to SharePoint and signed off with the HOTO Manager.

⚠️ The most common failure point

Option 43 not configured on the DHCP server. When an AP boots up it asks DHCP "where is my WLC?" — Option 43 holds the answer. If it's missing, the AP gets an IP address but never finds the WLC and sits there doing nothing. Always verify this before migration day, and raise an Option 43 request if it isn't set.

🗓️ The four phases

Ordering (GOLD/SALTO orders, EDD, warehouse) → Pre-Migration (staging, Option 43, MACHX, UAT doc, change request) → During Migration (cutover & UAT) → Post-Migration (notifications, CMDB/DNAC, close orders, HOTO). Ordering + Pre-Migration typically run 3–5 weeks; the cutover itself is usually a few hours to a day.

Who's who: DPM = you (Delivery Project Manager) · VPO = the operations engineer who stages and migrates the devices · SC = Solution Consultant (runbook + LLD) · ODM = Order Delivery Manager · PM = Project Manager · HOTO = Hand-Over-To-Operations.
